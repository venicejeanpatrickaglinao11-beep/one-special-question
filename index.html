A LITTLE NOTE FROM MY HEART

Dear Pretty,

Hi Lite. I wanted to tell you this for a while now, I'm so sorry I've taken so long. I often feel like I have nothing to give you and I am not doing enough to make you proud. Sometimes I feel like I need to be a better person to give you more.

But I have realised that I shouldn't wait for the 'perfect' time. Even though I may not have everything I want, I know how I feel. I know that I want to give you my time, effort and future.

I have realised how much I want you in my life! I love how you play and act silly. I love your stories and just listening to you. Whether it be a serious topic or not, I love knowing what's going on in your head. I love having simple conversations and giggling. It's funny how small things make me so happy, as I get to spend time with you. These small things mean the world to me and I want more of you in my life.

I want you to know that I really want you. I want to be someone you can trust. Someone to share your happiness, worries and successes with.

Although I have a lot to learn and I will never be perfect, I will put the effort in to understand you, listen and improve myself. I don't want to just be someone who makes you happy with words, but I want to show you with my actions that you are loved, valued, understood and respected.

I can't promise that our relationship will be perfect (they never are!), but I can promise that I will always put 100% effort into our relationship. I will always communicate with you during tough times, understand you and make sure you never feel like you're the only one putting in effort.

I want to grow with you. I want to chase our personal dreams and support each other throughout life. I want to celebrate our small successes. I want to enjoy the small things in life with you. I want to hear more of your stories, giggle about small things and listen to your random thoughts. I want to constantly learn more about what makes you YOU!

Most importantly, I want you to know that you don't have to go it alone. I want to be someone you can turn to, especially during tough times. I want us to be a comfort, support and safe place for each other.

Even though I may not be able to give you everything you deserve right now, I hope you will let me show you that I am willing to put the effort in, improve myself and be someone you can be proud of. I can't promise you a problem-free life, but I can promise that I am always willing to face life's challenges with you and work towards the life we've always dreamed of.

I have thought about this a lot and I don't want to hold these feelings back anymore. I want to be clear with my intentions and finally ask...

With all my sincerity,
Junjun ♡
